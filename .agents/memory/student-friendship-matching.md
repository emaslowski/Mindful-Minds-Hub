The user chose a real friendship matcher below the Blog on Connect, not in the Create art section. Preserve its opt-in, enrolled-individual-only scope. Use bounded selectable traits, limit identity exposure, and avoid direct messaging or contact details. Every student who asks to join needs teacher approval before they see or appear in matches; leaving and joining again requires a new approval.

**Why:** The audience is fifth graders. The user explicitly chose real student-to-student matching and later explicitly requested teacher approval for every student asking to join. Open profiles or unsupervised contact would create avoidable privacy and safety risks.

**How to apply:** When expanding matching or social features, keep individual enrollment and active-account checks separate from shared Studio job-group identities. Enforce approval on the server before showing either side of a match; do not treat a profile submission as approval. Prefer teacher-facilitated introductions over direct contact between students.
