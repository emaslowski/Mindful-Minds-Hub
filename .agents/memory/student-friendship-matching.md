The user chose a real friendship matcher below the Blog on Connect, not in the Create art section. It is open to individually enrolled students and students with an active teacher-issued Journal account; shared Studio job-group identities are not eligible. Use bounded selectable traits, limit identity exposure, and avoid direct messaging or contact details. Every student who asks to join needs a separate Matchmaker teacher approval before they see or appear in matches; leaving and joining again requires a new approval.

Approval is tied to the peer-visible identity: changing that identity is a new moderation decision, unlike editing interests.

**Why:** The audience is fifth graders. The user explicitly chose real student-to-student matching, teacher approval for every student asking to join, and later confirmed that a Journal login alone is enough to request Matchmaker access. Open profiles or unsupervised contact would create avoidable privacy and safety risks. A changed display name could otherwise bypass the identity the teacher approved.

**How to apply:** When expanding matching or social features, validate either an active enrolled individual or an active Journal credential without treating shared Studio job-group sessions as individuals. Enforce Matchmaker approval on the server before showing either side of a match; a Journal approval or profile submission is not Matchmaker approval. Require fresh review for changed public identity, but not for ordinary trait edits. Prefer teacher-facilitated introductions over direct contact between students.
