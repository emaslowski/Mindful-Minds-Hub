---
name: Teacher publication control
description: Teacher-controlled website editions and separation from original student submissions.
---

Teachers must be able to edit how a project appears on its destination website
page before publishing, and revise that public edition later.

**Why:** The user said teacher Review & Publish would not publish, and asked to
edit the project to make it look exactly how they want on the website page.
They clarified that this includes "videos and pictures and recipes, etc.",
not only article text and formatting.

**How to apply:** Keep the students' original work intact. Teacher draft saves
must not silently change an already published page; only an explicit publish
or update action changes the public edition. Use the same rendering for the
teacher preview and the website so formatting choices match.

Teachers want to post separately in each Movement drill/activity section.
Directed Draws videos should play directly on the illustrated easel, not require
opening a separate story first.

**Why:** The user asked to post in each different Movement drill section and
to put a video right on the Directed Draws easel.

**How to apply:** Preserve section-specific placement and the normal explicit
teacher publishing step; uploading or saving alone must not expose a draft.

Verify all teacher decisions from the Teacher Reviewing state, not just the
publish path. Clearly distinguish preview verification from a live-site fix.

**Why:** The user reported that none of the review buttons worked after preview
publishing had been verified, while the live site still ran the older code.

**How to apply:** Check the live deployment and its errors when this recurs.
Use disposable development projects to verify decisions; never change real
student submissions merely to test the buttons.

## Preview copies of live publications

When the user requests preview copies, copy only the already-published edition,
not private student drafts, accounts, or assignments. Give copied media its own
storage object rather than sharing the live object's path.

**Why:** The user wanted existing live work visible in the redesign preview
without changing the originals. Independent media prevents later preview edits
or deletions from affecting a live image.

**How to apply:** Import into development only, keep the published text and
appearance intact, and verify both the copied media and the unchanged live piece.

## Student artwork and monthly recognition

Students can submit photos of their artwork beneath the directed draw videos.
Keep posting directions beside the submission form. Each month's winner is
the website's Featured Artist of the Month.

**Why:** The user requested student artwork uploads under the directed draws,
instructions for posting, and a featured artist each month.

**How to apply:** Preserve teacher approval before student photos appear
publicly, and teacher control of the monthly winner. Do not choose winners
automatically or introduce student voting without a new request.

## Gardening video priority

On the Gardening page, keep student videos large and before the supplied
activities.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked for the student gardening video to be
"bigger and first," followed by more things for students to do.

**How to apply:** Preserve this student-first emphasis when adding or
rearranging Gardening activities. Do not change the original published media
or teacher-approved edition just to enlarge its page presentation.

## Food Reviews placement

Give Food Reviews its own button in the Cooking Corner, separate from Cooking
Videos and Recipes, with food review videos inside that view.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a new Food Reviews button after the
standalone section did not provide the placement they wanted.

**How to apply:** Preserve this separate category when revising Cooking Corner
navigation and keep the normal teacher-controlled publication workflow.

## Active Challenge photos and recognition

Students can share photos of themselves doing the Active Challenge. The
Exercise Center should look clean and less busy, and include Athlete of the Month.
Keep teacher approval before photos become public and teacher selection of the
monthly athlete, consistent with student artwork recognition.

**Why:** The user asked for a cleaner exercise section because “there's a lot
going on,” a place to upload Active Challenge photos, and an Athlete of the Month.

**How to apply:** Keep uploads associated with Active Challenge, retain existing
exercise destinations, and preserve privacy and teacher publication controls.
Put the Active Challenge video first, before photo uploads and the gallery,
as the user explicitly requested.

## Podcast collection

The Brightside Podcast entry opens a collection of all published podcast episodes,
including video podcasts about sports. Keep sports podcasts in the Podcast
destination rather than among exercise drills.

**Why:** The user asked to see all podcasts when clicking the Brightside Podcast
and to put the sports video podcast there.

**How to apply:** Preserve teacher-controlled publishing, support both audio and
video episodes, and check existing publication placement before moving media.

## Homepage video placement

Keep the user-supplied homepage video directly above “Find your bright spot.”

**Why:** The user explicitly requested that placement for their uploaded video.

**How to apply:** Preserve the full recording and its audio, provide playback
controls without autoplay, and keep large video files in App Storage rather
than bundling them into the website.

The user also requested a slightly smaller player and closed captions.
Keep captions available when resizing or replacing this homepage video.
