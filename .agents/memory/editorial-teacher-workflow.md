---
name: Editorial and teacher review workflow
description: User's intended review order, compliment-sandwich feedback, and removal of a separate drafting stage.
---

Students submit projects for peer review to the Editors & Designers Command Center. Editors and designers review the project and give constructive criticism using a compliment sandwich: an opening compliment, a constructive suggestion, and an encouraging closing. They then send the project and feedback to the Teacher Command Center.

**Why:** The user wants to review projects after editorial feedback, rather than letting editorial reviewers return projects directly to authors.

**How to apply:** A reviewer's revision recommendation is advice for the teacher, not permission to skip teacher review. Preserve previous feedback when the teacher requests revisions and a project goes through another review round.

Do not include Drafting as a separate student workflow stage. Keep the content editor, preview, sources, and saved work.

**Why:** The user said the drafting section seems pointless and asked to get rid of it.

**How to apply:** Students can work on content during planning/research and submit directly to peer review. Preserve compatibility with older projects without making students go through an extra drafting step.
