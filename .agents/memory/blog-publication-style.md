---
name: Blog publication style
description: Teacher's requested presentation for actual blog writing, covers, and examples.
---

Full published blog stories should look like complete writing pieces, with a calming
image above the article, rather than a worksheet divided by section headings.
Keep one main article title and readable paragraphs. Do not show the sample
blog posts.

**Why:** The user said they disliked the headings in the published post and
wanted "a full writing piece without being broken up," with a calming image
like the examples, but without the sample articles themselves.

**How to apply:** Keep the original student submission and teacher draft
unchanged; this is a presentation rule for the blog and its matching preview.
Planning fields such as topic and target reader are not part of the finished
writing. Do not apply this restriction to recipe cards or other destinations.

The blog listing should show compact story previews, with students clicking
“Read the full story” to open the complete article instead of displaying every
full article in the listing.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to make the blog post smaller and let students
click to read the full story.

**How to apply:** Keep the existing full-story presentation and all writing intact;
change only the listing and how readers open the complete article.
