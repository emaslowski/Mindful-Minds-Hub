---
name: Student workflow recovery
description: Product intent behind returning to the beginning after an accidental research-stage change.
---

Treat returning to the beginning as correcting an accidental stage change, not restarting or deleting a project.

**Why:** The user asked for students to return to the beginning of working on their content if they accidentally move into researching.

**How to apply:** Preserve the student's existing content, notes, and saved sources. Keep early-stage recovery separate from later review and publishing approvals.
